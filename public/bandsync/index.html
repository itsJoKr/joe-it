BandSync: Chords & Setlists

One phone leads, the whole band follows. BandSync keeps everyone in the rehearsal room on the same page — the leader picks a song or jumps to the second chorus, and it opens on every phone at once. Paste any chord sheet off the web to get a clean chart, then build setlists, transpose, set a capo, and autoscroll. Followers just scan a QR code — no account needed.

How it works

  1. Create a band and add your songs.

  3. The others scan it. No account needed.
  4. Pick a song — it opens on every phone in the room.
  5. Jump to the second chorus — the whole band goes with you.
